## Important payment reference

Use the parent's **surname** as the payment reference so we can match the
payment to the correct account, for example:

    Reference: REYNEKE

Payments without a clear reference may be delayed.

## Once you have paid

1. Keep your bank receipt / proof-of-payment.
2. On the app's Payment screen, upload the proof-of-payment image (camera or
   gallery). A photo of the screen/bank statement is fine.
3. Our finance team will verify the payment and update your application within
   1 business day. You will see the status change to "Payment Verified".
